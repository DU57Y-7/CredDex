CredDex

Your personal collection of micro-credentials.

CredDex is a free web app for collecting, organising and showing off the micro-credentials, badges and certificates you earn through study, work and volunteering.

  • Build an unlimited personal collection, with your own tags, search and filters.
  • Keep badge artwork and evidence links together in one place.
  • Optionally share a public profile link, or embed your collection on your own website.
  • Sign in with Google or an email address. Export your data at any time.
