How it works

  1. Send us your details. Fill in the order form with your organisation, your badge names, what people do to earn each one, and your artwork.
  2. We set it up. We register your organisation and create each badge, with its own information page and a way to contact you.
  3. You issue it. Your team pastes in email addresses and the badge is issued. There is no limit on how many people can earn it.
  4. They claim and share. Each person signs in to CredDex with that email, adds the badge to their collection, then downloads it, copies the link, or adds it to LinkedIn.
  5. Anyone can check it. Employers open the link and see who issued the badge, when, and whether it is still valid. No sign-in needed.

What’s included

  • A public information page for every badge, with your description, criteria and a contact button
  • A verification link for every badge issued, which shows valid, revoked or expired
  • A downloadable badge image with the badge details built in
  • Revoking a badge at any time, and optional expiry dates
  • Your badges listed on the CredDex home page, if you choose

CredDex is free for students and members to use.

Questions

How many people can earn a badge?

As many as you like. The $50 is per credential, not per person.

What do you need from us?

The details on the order form, and your artwork as a PNG or JPG. Your team issues badges using the email addresses of the people who earned them.

What about students under 18?

You are responsible for having any consent you need before you give us email addresses. A person’s name never appears on a badge page.

How do I pay?

We confirm your order and send an invoice. Our contact details and payment details are on the order form.
