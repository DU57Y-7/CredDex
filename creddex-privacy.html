CredDex privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address, and the name and profile details Google provides if you sign in with Google.
  • The credentials you add: names, issuers, dates, descriptions, tags, evidence links and badge images.
  • Your chosen username and display name.
  • Optional details for recommendations: your institution, field of study or work, status (for example "University student"), and the skill areas you pick as goals.
  • The category you give a credential, whether it is marked as a past event or one-off, and the suggestions you choose to hide (with an optional reason).
  • When you agreed to our terms.

How we use it

We use this information only to run CredDex: to sign you in and to store and show your collection. Google user data is used only for signing in. We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

The optional recommendation details, together with the tags and categories on your credentials, are used only to rank suggestions from our catalogue. The matching happens in your browser. They are visible only to you and to the CredDex administrator, are never shown on your public profile, and aren't shared or used for advertising. You can change or clear them at any time in Profile & settings.

Badges issued to you through CredDex

Organisations such as schools, clubs and teams can issue badges to email addresses through CredDex. When that happens we store the email address the organisation entered, the badge, who issued it and when, and whether it has been claimed or revoked. The organisation and CredDex administrators can see those details. A badge only joins your collection when you sign in with that email address and choose to add it. Anyone with a badge's verification link can see the badge, who issued it and whether it is still valid. They see your name only if you have claimed it and made your profile public. Otherwise they see "a CredDex member". If you are under 18, the organisation is responsible for having any consent it needs before it gives us your email address.

Browsing without an account

You can browse credentials without signing in. We keep a random number in your browser so that suggestions appear in the same order each visit. It isn't linked to you.

Public profiles

Your collection is private by default. If you turn on a public profile, the credentials you haven't hidden are visible to anyone with your link, including on any website where you embed it.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage, Singapore region). The CredDex website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

You can edit or delete credentials at any time, hide individual credentials, make your profile private, clear your recommendation details and hidden suggestions, and export your data. To delete your account and all its data, email dustinjmcguire@gmail.com.
